# AuraBuds Pro — Product FAQ

## Resetting the device
To factory-reset AuraBuds Pro, place both earbuds in the case, keep the lid
open, and hold the case button for 10 seconds until the light flashes white.

## Battery life
AuraBuds Pro last up to 8 hours on a single charge, and up to 30 hours total
with the charging case.

## Water resistance
AuraBuds Pro are IPX4 rated — sweat and light-rain resistant, but not for
swimming or showering.

## Pairing with a new phone
Open the case near your phone, go to Bluetooth settings, and select
"AuraBuds Pro". If it doesn't appear, factory-reset first, then retry.

## Warranty
AuraBuds Pro include a 12-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
