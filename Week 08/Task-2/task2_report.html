Verdict & recommendation

RAG works, and it is measurably better where it matters. Retrieval accuracy was perfect on the policy corpus (4/4 correct document cited); RAG beat a direct LLM on every private-fact question and correctly stayed inside the document boundary on q10 where the direct model did not. Hallucinations were zero, now enforced by a contextual-grounding guardrail that intervened once (q9) without over-blocking.

Chunking: for this small, distinct corpus, fixed-size is sufficient — document retrieval was tied at 100%. But hierarchical returned 3–6.5× more complete context per chunk on the long whitepaper, which produces better-grounded answers. Recommendation: fixed-size for short-document corpora; switch to hierarchical as documents grow longer and context completeness matters.
